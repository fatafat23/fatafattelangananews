About Us

Fatafat Telangana News is an independent digital newsroom covering all 33 districts of Telangana and every Indian state. Our reporting focuses on fast, fact-checked updates — breaking news, district developments, jobs, business, cricket, politics and weather.

We publish in English with Telugu support, and bring you live news feeds along with original video reports and reels.

Terms of Use

By using this website you agree that all content, including live news feeds and original reporting, is provided for personal, non-commercial use. You may not republish, scrape or redistribute our content without written permission. Third-party links lead to external sites governed by their own terms.

Privacy Policy

We do not sell personal data. We use basic browser storage (localStorage) only to cache live quotes so the site does not re-fetch on every visit. No login is required and no personal accounts are created on this site.

Corrections Policy

We are committed to accuracy. If a story, headline or feed item contains an error, email fatafattelangananews@gmail.com with “Correction” in the subject. Verified corrections are updated promptly.

Disclaimer

Market and weather data are indicative only and not investment advice. Fatafat Telangana News does not independently verify every third-party item.
